abstract = "The emergence of bacterial antibiotic resistance is a growing
problem, yet the variables that influence the rate of emergence
of resistance are not well understood. In a microfluidic device
designed to mimic naturally occurring bacterial niches,
resistance of Escherichia coli to the antibiotic ciprofloxacin
developed within 10 hours. Resistance emerged with as few as 100
bacteria in the initial inoculation. Whole-genome sequencing of
the resistant organisms revealed that four functional
single-nucleotide polymorphisms attained fixation. Knowledge
about the rapid emergence of antibiotic resistance in the
heterogeneous conditions within the mammalian body may be helpful
in understanding the emergence of drug resistance during cancer
chemotherapy.",
